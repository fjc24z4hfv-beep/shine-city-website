Paver sealing

Clean, re-sand the joints, and seal. Driveways, pool decks, patios, and walkways across Orlando & the Space Coast.

What the work includes

Florida sun, afternoon rain, and irrigation stains fade pavers and wash sand out of the joints. Sealing is the maintenance step after a real clean, not a substitute for one.

A standard Shine City seal has three parts: we clean the surface, re-sand the joints with a joint sand that bonds with the sealer to lock the pavers in place, and seal in the finish you pick. Natural matte stays closer to the paver's dry color. The wet look deepens the color and adds a shine. Tell us which you want when we look at the job.

We seal driveways, pool decks, patios, and walkways. If the old sealer is hazy, peeling, or sticky, that is sealer stripping: $2.25/sq ft (strip only; re-sand and reseal not included).

How a sealing job goes

  1. Instant estimate

    Send your address and up to 6 photos from different angles, including the whole driveway or patio.

    Photos from several angles, plus the satellite view, give a more accurate size.

    Get your estimate

  2. Free on-site look

    We confirm the square footage and the price.

    We check the pavers and the joints so the final price is right.

  3. Clean and re-sand

    We clean the pavers, then re-sand the joints with a joint sand that bonds with the sealer and locks the pavers in place.

    That lock helps the joints resist weeds and washout.

  4. Seal

    Natural matte or wet look, once the pavers are ready.

    Sealer protects the color from Florida sun and stains: matte keeps a natural look. The wet look deepens the color and adds a shine.
