Paver sealing

Clean, re-sand the joints, and seal. Driveways, pool decks, patios, and walkways across Orlando & the Space Coast.

What the work includes

Florida sun, afternoon rain, and irrigation stains fade pavers and wash sand out of the joints. Sealing is the maintenance step after a real clean, not a substitute for one.

A standard Shine City seal has three parts: we clean the surface, re-sand the joints with polymeric sand, and seal in the finish you pick. Natural matte stays closer to the paver's dry color. Wet look deepens the color and adds a sheen. Tell us which you want when we look at the job.

We seal driveways, pool decks, patios, and walkways. If the old sealer is hazy, peeling, or sticky, that is a stripping and resealing job, quoted on its own. New pavers belong in the First Seal Program so we don't seal over the white bloom.
