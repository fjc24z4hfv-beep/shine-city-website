Pressure washing & house washing

Paver sealing is the focus. We also wash the rest of the outside in Orlando & the Space Coast.

House washing

Siding, soffits, and the outside of the house pick up mildew in our humidity. House washing is a wash for those surfaces. It is not a close-range blast across painted walls or screens.

Pressure washing

We pressure wash concrete driveways, sidewalks, and pool decks. If the surface is pavers, a wash alone will not re-sand the joints or seal them. For pavers, start with paver sealing. If an old coat has failed, see stripping and resealing.

Price

House washing and pressure washing do not have a published price. They are not part of the paver sealing square-foot rates. Tell us what you want washed and we'll quote it. The quote is free and there is no obligation. Call or text 321-795-0313.
