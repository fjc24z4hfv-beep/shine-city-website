Pressure washing

Driveways, sidewalks, walkways, patios, pavers, and pool decks in Orlando & Central Florida.

What pressure washing does

Dirt, grime, mildew, and irrigation stains

We pressure wash driveways, sidewalks, walkways, patios, pavers, and pool decks. Florida's heat and humidity leave dirt, grime, mildew, and irrigation stains on those surfaces. The wash lifts that buildup off.

Even finish, no stripes

On flat concrete and pavers, a surface cleaner covers the surface in an even pass. That gives a consistent finish and avoids the stripes a wand can leave.

Curb appeal

A stained driveway or a dark walk is the first thing people see. Cleaning it makes the front of the house and the paths around it look kept up.

The first step before paver sealing

Cleaning is also the first step before paver sealing. When the pavers need sand and a sealer after the wash, that work is on the paver sealing page.

Less of a slip hazard

Algae on pool decks and walkways gets slick underfoot. Pressure washing that film off makes those surfaces less of a slip hazard.
