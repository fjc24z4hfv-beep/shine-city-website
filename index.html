How a sealing job goes

The same steps, whether the pavers are new or they've been down for years.

  1. Tell us about the pavers

    Call, text, or send the form. The street address helps us see if neighbors can book together.

  2. Free on-site look

    We confirm the square footage and the price. On new pavers this is the Shine Check. If the white bloom is still active, we wait.

  3. Clean

    Dirt, stains, and growth come off so the sealer goes onto a clean surface.

  4. Re-sand the joints

    We re-sand the joints with polymeric sand so the sand stays put between the pavers.

  5. Seal

    You pick the finish: natural matte or wet look. We seal when the pavers are ready, not just to hit a date.

Questions

What does paver sealing include?

We clean the pavers, re-sand the joints with polymeric sand, and seal them. You choose a natural matte finish or a wet look. That covers driveways, pool decks, patios, and walkways.

How do the two rates work?

Jobs of 800 sq ft or less are $2.00 per sq ft. Jobs over 800 sq ft are $1.75 per sq ft, and that lower rate applies to the whole job, not just the part past 800. Those rates are for paver sealing: clean, re-sand joints, and seal. The final price is confirmed after a free on-site look.

When should new pavers be sealed?

New concrete pavers release a white bloom called efflorescence for weeks or months. Seal over it too soon and the haze can get trapped. Paver makers often suggest waiting at least 30 to 60 days, and in Florida many jobs wait longer. We check yours and seal when the bloom has passed.

What if the old sealer is hazy or peeling?

That is stripping and resealing, which is quoted separately from the published paver sealing rates. More coats on top of a failed sealer usually make the haze worse. We look first, then quote the strip and the new seal.

Do neighbors save if we book together?

Yes. Save when your neighbors book. We don't list a percentage on this site. Ask when you request the quote and we'll explain the savings for your street.
