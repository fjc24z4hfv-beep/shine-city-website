Questions

What does the price include?

Paver sealing: we clean the pavers, re-sand the joints with polymeric sand, and seal them. You choose a natural matte finish or a wet look. Driveways, pool decks, patios, and walkways.

How do the two rates work?

The first 800 sq ft are $2.00 per sq ft. Only the square footage above 800 is $1.75 per sq ft. A 1,000 sq ft job is $1,600 plus 200 sq ft at $1.75, which is $1,950. There is no job minimum. The final price is confirmed after a free on-site look.

When should new pavers be sealed?

New pavers release a white bloom called efflorescence. Seal over it too soon and the haze can get trapped. We check yours and seal when the bloom has passed. First Seal Program

What if the old sealer is hazy or peeling?

That is stripping and resealing, quoted separately from the rates above. Stripping and resealing
