Soft washing

Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution to remove algae, mildew, and dirt from stucco, siding, stone veneer, and screen enclosures. Florida's heat and humidity make that growth spread fast, but soft washing kills it at the root, so it doesn't come back as quickly, and it does it without the surface damage high pressure can cause.
