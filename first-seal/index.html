The First Seal Program

Your first seal, done right.

New-build pavers in Orlando & the Space Coast. A free Shine Check now. The seal happens after the white bloom has passed.

Why new pavers should wait

A new driveway can look finished the week you move in. The concrete in the pavers is often still pushing minerals to the surface. That white film is efflorescence. It can show up right away or months later, especially after rain.

If sealer goes on while the bloom is still working its way out, the white haze can get locked underneath. Clearing that up usually means stripping the sealer and starting over. Waiting too long has its own cost: Florida sun, rain, oil, and irrigation stains soak into bare pavers, and joint sand starts to wash out.

Paver manufacturers often say to wait at least 30 to 60 days after installation. Many Florida jobs wait longer than that, sometimes through a rainy stretch so the bloom can finish. We don't promise a calendar date. We check the pavers and seal when they're actually ready.

How the program works

  1. Free Shine Check

    We look at the pavers, note about when they were installed, and see whether the white bloom is still active. You can start with photos, and we'll still confirm on site before any seal.

  2. We wait if we need to

    If the bloom is still coming out, we don't seal just to fill a slot on the calendar. We'll tell you what we see and when to have us back.

  3. Clean, re-sand, seal

    When the bloom has passed, we clean, re-sand the joints with polymeric sand, and seal in a natural matte or wet look.

What it costs

The Shine Check is free. The sealing itself uses the published paver sealing rates: clean, re-sand joints, and seal, at $2.00 per sq ft up to 800 sq ft and $1.75 per sq ft over 800. The final price is confirmed after we look.
